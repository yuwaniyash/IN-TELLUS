Energy audit and energy-monitoring service providers help organizations establish energy baselines, identify significant energy uses and verify whether interventions are producing the expected results. Suitable providers may offer metering, sub-metering, data analysis, energy audits, monitoring systems and measurement-and-verification support. Agent 3 should recommend this category when a customer needs measured evidence before committing to larger efficiency investments. Provider selection should consider technical qualifications, measurement capability, relevant facility experience and the ability to produce documented findings rather than relying only on projected savings. Source basis: SLSEA energy-management and benchmarking guidance.
