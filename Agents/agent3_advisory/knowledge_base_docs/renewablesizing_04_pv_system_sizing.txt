Rooftop PV system sizing should be based on actual electricity demand and the characteristics of the proposed installation rather than a universal kW-per-square-metre rule. A sizing assessment should consider electricity consumption patterns, available installation area, solar resource, shading, inverter capacity, electrical constraints, and the intended grid-interconnection arrangement. SLSEA's rooftop PV installation guideline is intended to support planning and installation of rooftop PV systems in Sri Lanka and assessment of their technical compatibility. Effort level: medium to high because the final size requires site-specific engineering analysis. Source basis: SLSEA Guideline on Rooftop Solar PV Installation in Sri Lanka.
