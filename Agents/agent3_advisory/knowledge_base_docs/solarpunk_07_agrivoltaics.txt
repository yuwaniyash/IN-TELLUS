Agrivoltaics combines solar PV with agricultural activities such as crop production, livestock grazing or pollinator habitat on the same land. Research from the U.S. Department of Energy describes agrivoltaics as an active area of research and identifies potential benefits and trade-offs involving agricultural production, energy production, land use and system design. The suitability of an agrivoltaic project depends on crop or livestock requirements, panel configuration, access, shading, irrigation, structural design and local economics. Planning level: transformative and high effort; this recommendation is a planning estimate, not a certified engineering assessment. Source basis: U.S. Department of Energy agrivoltaics research and guidance.
