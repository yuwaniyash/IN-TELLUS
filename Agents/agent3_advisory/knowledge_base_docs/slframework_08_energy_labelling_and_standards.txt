Sri Lanka has an appliance energy labelling and energy-performance standards programme administered through the Sri Lanka Sustainable Energy Authority. Published standards cover products including LED lamps, electric motors, refrigerators and air conditioners, with different labelling or standards status depending on the product. SLSEA also identifies mandatory energy labelling for certain products, including LED lamps under the relevant Sri Lankan standard and regulation. Agent 3 should distinguish between a product being energy efficient in general and a product meeting a specific Sri Lankan standard or labelling requirement. Authority level: official Sri Lankan framework. Source basis: SLSEA energy performance standards and appliance labelling programme.
