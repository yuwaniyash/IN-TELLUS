Sri Lanka's National Green Reporting System (NGRS) is a national sustainability reporting framework implemented through the Ministry of Environment. The revised NGRS guidelines were developed as a second version because the earlier framework did not adequately reflect current global sustainability reporting expectations. The 2026 revised guidelines are aligned with the Global Reporting Initiative's latest guidelines and internationally used sustainability reporting frameworks. NGRS is intended to help organizations quantify and report sustainability performance and support continual improvement. Authority level: official Sri Lankan government framework. Source basis: Ministry of Environment, Revised National Green Reporting System of Sri Lanka Reporting Guidelines, 2026.
