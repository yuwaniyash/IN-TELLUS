Green roof systems combine vegetation with a building's roof structure, providing insulation that reduces cooling load while also managing stormwater runoff - relevant for facilities in urban areas with limited ground-level land for other interventions. Community or facility-level microgrids pair solar generation with battery storage to allow a facility to operate independently of grid supply during outages or peak-demand periods, and are typically considered at the concept stage for facilities with multiple buildings or sites that could share a shared power infrastructure.
