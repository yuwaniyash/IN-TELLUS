NGRS requires organizations to report greenhouse gas emissions separated into Scope 1 (direct emissions from sources owned or controlled by the organization, such as fuel combustion or generator use) and Scope 2/3 (indirect emissions from purchased electricity and value-chain activities). Organizations must disclose the emission factors and calculation methodology used, and the source of those factors. This structured Scope 1/2/3 reporting approach aligns with internationally recognized frameworks while being administered specifically for Sri Lankan organizations.
