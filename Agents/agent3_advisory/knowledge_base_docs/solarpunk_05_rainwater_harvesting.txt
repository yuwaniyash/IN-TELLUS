Rainwater harvesting collects rainfall from surfaces such as roofs and stores it for later use. A basic system requires a collection surface, storage vessel and defined end use, while larger systems may include filtration, treatment, pumps and distribution infrastructure. Non-potable uses such as irrigation, cleaning or toilet flushing may be considered where the system is properly designed, while potable use requires appropriate treatment and compliance with applicable requirements. Planning level: transformative and medium effort depending on system scale; this recommendation is a planning estimate, not a certified engineering assessment. Source basis: U.S. EPA green-infrastructure and rainwater-harvesting guidance.
