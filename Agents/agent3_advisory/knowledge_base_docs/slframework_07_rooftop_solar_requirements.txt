Sri Lanka has a rooftop solar framework supported by the Sri Lanka Sustainable Energy Authority, Ceylon Electricity Board and Lanka Electricity Company for connecting rooftop PV systems to the electricity network. SLSEA describes net metering, net accounting and net plus arrangements and provides technical documentation and requirements for rooftop PV installations. Solar PV installation services for the rooftop programme are required to be obtained from registered service providers. Agent 3 should treat current utility and SLSEA requirements as authoritative when discussing rooftop solar installation or grid connection and should not substitute generic international guidance for local requirements. Authority level: official Sri Lankan framework. Source basis: SLSEA Soorya Bala Sangramaya and rooftop solar programme documentation.
