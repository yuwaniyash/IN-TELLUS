Anaerobic digestion can process organic materials such as manure, food waste and wastewater solids to produce biogas and digestate. Biogas can be used for heat or electricity generation, while digestate may have value as a nutrient-rich material when appropriately managed. A viable project requires a sufficiently consistent feedstock, suitable digestion technology, gas handling, safety controls, residue management and an appropriate end use for the energy. Planning level: transformative and high effort; this recommendation is a planning estimate, not a certified engineering assessment. Source basis: U.S. EPA anaerobic-digestion guidance.
