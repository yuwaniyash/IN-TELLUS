LED lighting retrofits typically reduce lighting-related electricity consumption by 40-60% compared to fluorescent or incandescent fixtures, with payback periods commonly between 1-3 years depending on prior fixture type and operating hours. Facilities with high-occupancy areas running 12+ hours daily see the fastest payback. Beyond direct energy savings, LED retrofits reduce cooling load in air-conditioned spaces since LEDs emit significantly less heat than traditional fixtures, compounding the electricity savings in commercial buildings.
