Green roofs add a vegetated layer to a building roof and can provide environmental benefits including shading, heat reduction and stormwater management. EPA describes green roofs as one form of green infrastructure and notes that vegetation can help reduce heat-island effects by shading surfaces and releasing moisture. A green-roof project must assess structural capacity, waterproofing, drainage, vegetation, maintenance and local climate conditions before implementation. Planning level: transformative and medium to high effort; this recommendation is a planning estimate, not a certified engineering assessment. Source basis: U.S. EPA green-roof and green-infrastructure guidance.
