Battery storage sizing should start from the loads that actually need backup or time-shifting rather than selecting a battery only from the PV system size. The assessment should identify critical loads, their power demand, expected operating duration, battery usable energy, inverter requirements, charging sources, and the required operating strategy. Solar-plus-storage systems can improve resilience, but a rooftop PV system alone normally does not provide power during a grid outage because grid-connected systems are designed to shut down for safety unless appropriate equipment and storage are configured. Effort level: high because battery capacity, controls, protection and electrical integration require technical design. Source basis: U.S. Department of Energy solar resilience and battery guidance.
