Lighting retrofit suppliers and contractors can provide LED equipment, lighting controls, installation and commissioning services. A suitable project should consider existing lighting technology, illumination requirements, operating hours, controls, product performance and applicable Sri Lankan standards or labelling requirements. SLSEA publishes energy-performance standards and labelling information for LED products, so product compliance should be checked rather than selecting equipment only from a claimed efficiency percentage. Agent 3 should recommend the vendor category and evaluation criteria, not endorse a particular supplier. Source basis: SLSEA appliance energy labelling and standards information.
