Water-efficiency service providers can support leak detection, plumbing inspection, water metering, fixture upgrades, process-water assessment and reuse opportunities. The appropriate service depends on whether the customer's main issue is leakage, high fixture consumption, process water use, cooling systems or another water demand. Agent 3 should recommend this category when the customer needs site inspection or measurement before selecting an intervention. Selection should consider relevant plumbing or water-management experience, measurement capability, maintenance support and clear documentation of proposed work. Source basis: water-efficiency and facility-management technical guidance.
