Under Sri Lanka's National Green Reporting System (NGRS), administered by the Ministry of Environment, organizations are expected to report total energy consumption broken down by source: non-renewable primary energy (coal, diesel, LPG), renewable energy generated or purchased, and a breakdown between electrical and thermal energy. Organizations must also report an energy intensity ratio, calculated as total energy consumption divided by an organization-specific metric such as production volume, floor area, or revenue. A third indicator requires reporting the quantity and percentage of energy reduction achieved through conservation initiatives, compared against a stated baseline, along with which specific measures caused the reduction - such as process redesign, equipment retrofitting, or operational changes.
