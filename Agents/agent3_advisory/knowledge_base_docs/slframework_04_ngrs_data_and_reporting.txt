The revised National Green Reporting System provides a framework for organizations to quantify and report sustainability performance rather than treating sustainability only as a set of individual projects. The Ministry of Environment describes goals that include building organizational capacity to quantify and report sustainability performance and encouraging continual improvement beyond compliance. Organizations using Agent 3 for reporting support should therefore distinguish measured organizational information from recommendations or estimates generated by the advisory system. Reporting content should be based on available organizational evidence and the applicable NGRS guidance. Authority level: official Sri Lankan government framework. Source basis: Ministry of Environment NGRS 2026 guidelines.
