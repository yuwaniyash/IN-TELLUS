The financial evaluation of a rooftop solar PV system should consider the system's initial cost, expected electricity generation, electricity tariff, operating and maintenance costs, financing arrangements, applicable electricity-export or grid-interconnection arrangements, and the expected lifetime of the system. A simple payback period can be estimated by comparing the initial project cost with the expected annual financial benefit from reduced electricity purchases and, where applicable, income associated with electricity supplied to the grid. This result depends on the applicable tariff and electricity-export arrangement and should therefore be calculated using current project-specific information rather than a fixed universal figure. Partial-offset systems, sized around the facility's actual consumption profile rather than the maximum available roof area, generally offer a more favorable return given typical Sri Lankan grid-interconnection arrangements.
