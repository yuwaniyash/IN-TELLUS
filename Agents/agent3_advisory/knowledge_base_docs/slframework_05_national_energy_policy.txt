Sri Lanka's National Energy Policy and Strategies was published in 2019 and sets a policy framework for a sustainable, reliable and economically feasible energy supply. The policy addresses energy security, renewable energy, energy efficiency and the longer-term transition of the country's energy system. It also includes a Results Delivery Framework assigning responsibilities for implementing policy goals. Agent 3 should use the policy as national policy context rather than treating it as a site-specific engineering requirement. Authority level: official Sri Lankan government policy. Source basis: National Energy Policy and Strategies of Sri Lanka, 2019.
