Recurring spikes in fuel consumption, especially across consecutive billing periods, often point to generator degradation rather than genuinely higher operational need. Common causes include worn injectors, clogged air filters, delayed maintenance intervals, or incorrect fuel-air mixture calibration. A basic inspection and service - checking filters, fluid levels, and injector calibration - typically resolves a meaningful fuel efficiency loss within a short service window and costs a fraction of equipment replacement. Facilities running generators as primary or backup power should follow the manufacturer's recommended maintenance schedule and keep records of operating hours and fuel consumption to catch degradation trends before they compound into major spikes.
