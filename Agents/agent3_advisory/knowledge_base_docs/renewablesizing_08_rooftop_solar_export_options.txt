Sri Lankan rooftop solar customers may use different electricity settlement arrangements, including net metering, net accounting and net plus arrangements described by the Sri Lanka Sustainable Energy Authority. The appropriate arrangement depends on the customer's electricity use, generation profile and the applicable utility and programme requirements. Net metering credits exported electricity against consumption under the described scheme, while other arrangements handle generation and export differently. Agent 3 should describe these as available framework options and should not assume a particular tariff or financial outcome without checking current utility information. Effort level: medium because scheme selection should be verified against current requirements. Source basis: SLSEA Soorya Bala Sangramaya and rooftop solar programme information.
