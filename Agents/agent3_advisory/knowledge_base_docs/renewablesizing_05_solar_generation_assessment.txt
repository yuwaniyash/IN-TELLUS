Solar generation estimates should use location-specific solar-resource information together with the proposed PV system design. Sri Lanka has a Solar Resource Atlas because earlier solar-resource information did not provide sufficient spatial and temporal resolution for some planning applications. Generation estimates should therefore account for solar resource, system capacity, orientation, shading, equipment characteristics and expected system losses rather than assuming a fixed output for every location. Effort level: medium, with final estimates requiring technical modelling or qualified installer assessment. Source basis: SLSEA Solar Resource Atlas information and rooftop PV guidance.
