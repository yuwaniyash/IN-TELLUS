Solar PV service providers can support site assessment, PV system design, equipment supply, installation, commissioning and post-installation support. In Sri Lanka, SLSEA states that companies providing the complete rooftop solar service package must register as solar PV service providers to engage in rooftop solar installation under its programme. A customer-facing system should therefore treat "registered solar PV service provider" as a category and verify the provider's current registration status before making a real procurement decision. Selection should also consider technical capability, warranty terms, insurance, installation experience and after-sales support. Source basis: SLSEA Solar PV Service Provider requirements.
