HVAC efficiency service providers can support inspection, maintenance, controls review, setpoint and scheduling assessment, equipment performance checks and system optimization. Depending on the facility, the work may involve air-conditioning equipment, ventilation systems, controls, filters, ductwork or other components. Agent 3 should use this category when HVAC performance needs technical inspection rather than recommending a particular company. Selection criteria should include relevant HVAC experience, technician qualifications, maintenance capability, measurement methods and documentation of recommended work. Source basis: general technical guidance and Sri Lankan building energy-efficiency requirements.
