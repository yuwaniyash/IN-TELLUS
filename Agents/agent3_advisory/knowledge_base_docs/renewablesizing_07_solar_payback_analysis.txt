Solar payback should be calculated from the customer's actual project economics rather than using a universal payback period. The calculation should consider installed cost, expected electricity generation, electricity tariff or export arrangement, operation and maintenance costs, financing costs where applicable, equipment life and degradation assumptions, and the customer's actual electricity-use pattern. A simple first estimate can compare annual net financial benefit with initial project cost, but a detailed investment decision should use a site-specific cash-flow analysis. Effort level: medium to high because reliable payback requires actual quotations, tariffs and generation estimates. Source basis: SLSEA rooftop solar guidance and project documentation.
