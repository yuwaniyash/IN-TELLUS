Sri Lanka receives strong solar potential nationally, with annual Global Horizontal Irradiance ranging roughly from 1,247 to 2,106 kWh/m2 depending on region. As a practical sizing reference, a 1kWp rooftop solar system typically requires about 6.5 square meters of roof space and weighs approximately 80kg once installed. A mounting angle of roughly 7-12 degrees tilted toward the south is recommended for the Colombo region, with the steeper end of that range helping reduce dust accumulation. Partial offset systems, sized to cover 15-25% of consumption, are often a more practical starting recommendation than full offset, given lower upfront investment and simpler grid arrangements.
