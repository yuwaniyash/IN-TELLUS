HVAC systems typically account for 30-50% of a commercial building's electricity consumption, making them a high-impact target for efficiency improvements. Simple interventions include adjusting thermostat setpoints by 1-2 degrees, sealing air leaks around windows and doors, and scheduling HVAC operation to match actual occupancy hours rather than running continuously. More involved interventions, such as upgrading to variable-speed drives on compressors or improving insulation, typically require a longer-term budget allocation but can reduce HVAC-related consumption by 20-30%.
