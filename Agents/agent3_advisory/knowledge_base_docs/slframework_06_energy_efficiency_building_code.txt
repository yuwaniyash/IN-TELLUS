Sri Lanka's Energy Efficiency Building Code provides energy-efficiency requirements and guidance for building systems and equipment. The code covers areas including building elements, ventilation and air conditioning, lighting, service and water heating, and electrical and power distribution. The 2021 edition was compiled by the Sri Lanka Sustainable Energy Authority after revision of the earlier code. Where Agent 3 discusses regulatory or compliance-related building energy requirements, the actual applicable code provisions should be consulted rather than relying on a general recommendation. Authority level: official Sri Lankan energy-efficiency framework. Source basis: Sri Lanka Sustainable Energy Authority Energy Efficiency Building Code of Sri Lanka.
