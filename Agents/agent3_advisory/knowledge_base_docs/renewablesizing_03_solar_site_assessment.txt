Solar PV site assessment should be completed before recommending a rooftop system size. The assessment should consider the facility's historical electricity consumption, available roof or ground area, shading, roof orientation and condition, electrical connection, and applicable utility requirements. Sri Lanka's Solar Resource Atlas provides solar-resource information that can support site assessment, but actual system generation still depends on the specific site and system design. Effort level: medium, because a reliable assessment requires site measurements and technical review. Source basis: Sri Lanka Sustainable Energy Authority (SLSEA) Solar Energy resources and rooftop PV guidance.
