A microgrid is a localized electrical system that combines loads with local energy resources such as solar generation, batteries and other distributed energy resources. Microgrids can operate while connected to the main grid and can be designed to operate in an islanded mode during an outage, subject to appropriate electrical protection and controls. Because microgrids involve generation, storage, control, protection and grid-interconnection considerations, they require detailed technical and economic design rather than a simple equipment recommendation. Planning level: transformative and high effort; this recommendation is a planning estimate, not a certified engineering assessment. Source basis: U.S. Department of Energy microgrid systems and solar-integration guidance.
