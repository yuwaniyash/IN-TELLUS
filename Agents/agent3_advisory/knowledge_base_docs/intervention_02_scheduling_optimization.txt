Facilities that run generators or high-draw equipment on fixed schedules regardless of actual demand often waste 15-20% of fuel or electricity on idle-time operation. Reviewing shift patterns and equipment run-times against actual usage data can identify windows where equipment could be powered down or throttled without affecting operations. This typically requires a one-to-three month usage audit comparing logged operating hours against production output, followed by adjusting start/stop schedules or installing automatic shutoff controls for idle periods exceeding a set threshold.
